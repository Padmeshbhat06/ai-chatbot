Campus Life

SVIT Flagship Campus Events

Participate in national-level hackathons, technical festivals, and cultural celebrations at Sai Vidya Institute of Technology.

National-Level Hackathon
Annual Flagship Event

HackFest

SVIT's flagship National-Level Hackathon bringing together student innovators and developers from across the country for an intensive 24-to-36-hour coding marathon to build real-world solutions in AI, Web/Mobile, Sustainability, and Smart Systems, complete with industry mentorship, live pitching, and cash prizes.

Annual Technical Fest
Inter-Collegiate Tech Festival

TechVidya

Sai Vidya Institute of Technology's annual inter-collegiate technical festival featuring competitive coding, robotics challenges, project expos, technical paper presentations, circuit debugging, hands-on workshops, and tech quizzes across all engineering branches.

Cultural & Heritage Fest
Kannada Cultural Extravaganza

SKU – Siri Kannada Utsava

SVIT's grand annual Kannada cultural extravaganza celebrating Karnataka's rich heritage, art, literature, music, folk and classical dance, theatre, and student creativity through vibrant inter-departmental and inter-collegiate cultural competitions.

State-Level Cultural Fest
April 16–18, 2026

Sanchalana Samvega 2026

Annual state-level cultural festival featuring music, dance, literary, arts, tech/gaming, and sports competitions alongside live concerts featuring Raghu Dixit and DJ Arun productions.
